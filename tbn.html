About TBN

A non-profit built on service, standing beside communities that need it most

TBN Treasure By Nuga is dedicated to improving lives through humanitarian services, education, youth empowerment, healthcare support, community development and sustainable initiatives. We believe every individual deserves the opportunity to thrive, regardless of their background.

Our mission is to create lasting impact by empowering individuals, supporting families, and building stronger, more resilient communities — one intervention at a time.

🎯 Our Mission

To uplift underserved communities through compassion, education, healthcare, empowerment and sustainable development.

🌅 Our Vision

A world where every individual has equal access to opportunity, dignity, hope and a better quality of life.

Compassion Integrity Accountability Service Excellence Inclusion Sustainability
What we do

Six programs, one shared purpose

From classrooms to clinics, our work meets people where they are and equips them for what's next.

📚

Education Support

Scholarships, school supplies, mentorship and learning opportunities for children and young adults.

🏘️

Community Development

Projects that improve sanitation, infrastructure and everyday living conditions.

🚀

Youth Empowerment

Skill acquisition, entrepreneurship training, leadership development and career mentorship.

🩺

Healthcare Outreach

Free medical outreaches, health awareness campaigns, maternal care and essential medical assistance.

🎒

Food & Relief Support

Food items, clothing and relief materials for vulnerable families in emergencies.

👜

Women Empowerment

Vocational training, financial literacy, business grants and one-on-one mentorship.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible? +
Depending on your jurisdiction, contributions to TBN may qualify for tax relief. We issue a donation receipt for every gift on request.
How is my donation used? +
Funds are allocated directly to our six core programs — education, healthcare, youth empowerment, relief, women empowerment and community development — with full transparency in our annual report.
Can I volunteer remotely? +
Yes — we welcome remote volunteers for content, design, fundraising and administrative support alongside our on-ground teams.
Do you publish impact reports? +
Absolutely. Annual reports covering our reach, spending and outcomes are available on request and published on this site.
